Campus Traces

This is an archive of traces of human activity. It records the evidence and changes of human presence through overlooked traces of activities left at ordinary campus spaces - a narrative of campus life beyond official documentations. How does this narrative shapes our living and working space? How does it shape society in a broader sense, through unnoticed actions accumulated over time?
